Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Who owns the ad account?

You do. It is created in your name with your billing, and if you leave you keep the conversion history and audiences. Ask any agency this before signing — the answer tells you a great deal.

Can you guarantee leads?

No, and nobody honest can. Results depend on your offer, your pricing, your website, your competition and platform changes outside anyone's control. We will tell you what is realistic and then report what actually happened.

Why fix tracking before spending?

Because without it you are buying clicks and guessing at results. Fixing measurement first sometimes reveals that previously reported successes were counting the wrong action entirely.

Can you say we are the best in the area?

Only if you can evidence it. The CAP Code requires documentary proof for objective claims, and a complaint lands on you as the advertiser. We will write something strong that you can actually stand behind.

Do you need long contracts?

No. Monthly, and you can leave with your accounts and data intact. An agency needing a twelve-month tie-in is protecting itself from its own results.

Whose job is cookie consent?

Yours — you are the data controller. We will tell you what tracking requires and what your consent mechanism and privacy notice need to cover, but the obligation stays with you.
